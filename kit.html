For parents

How to help without nagging.

You don't need to remember any GCSE science. Your job is to make revision feel normal, not to check up on it.

Say this

"What's one thing you remembered today?"

Recalling it out loud is extra revision, and it starts a conversation instead of an argument.

Say this

"Can you teach me how that works?"

Explaining to someone who doesn't know is one of the best ways to learn. Being the student is a gift to them.

Say this

"How many reds have you turned green?"

It shows interest in progress, not hours. Celebrate every red that turns amber or green.

Avoid this

"Have you done your revision?"

It invites a one-word answer and puts them on the defensive. Ask about what they learned, not whether they did it. And protect 30 focused minutes a day rather than pushing for more hours.
