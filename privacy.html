The short version

  • We collect only what we need to run your study plan.

Who we are

Stoday Plus is run by Mikey Hoang, trading as Stoday Plus, based in the United Kingdom. We are the "controller" of your personal data, which means we decide how it is used and are responsible for keeping it safe.

Contact: ngoclong003vn@gmail.com

A note for students and parents

Stoday Plus is designed for students aged 13 and over. If you are under 13, you need a parent or guardian to set up and manage your account. We follow the UK GDPR and the ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code (the "Children's Code"), which means privacy-protective settings are on by default and we don't use your data for advertising or profiling.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted services to run Stoday Plus. They only process your data on our instructions:

  • Supabase: stores your account and study data on servers in London, UK.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the website and counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics (see below).
  • Google: stores sign-up form emails (Google Sheets), serves the website's fonts, and lets you sign in with your Google account if you choose to.
  • Groq: generates replies in our AI tools. Text you send to an AI tool is processed in the United States. Please don't type personal information (like your full name or address) into AI tools.
  • Stripe: takes payments for Plus and lets you manage or cancel your subscription. Your card details go straight to Stripe; we never see them. Stripe may process data outside the UK, under approved safeguards (see below).

Where data is processed outside the UK, we rely on approved safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US Data Bridge.

We will never sell your data or share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-up form emails: until you unsubscribe or create an account, and at most 12 months.
  • Account and study data: for as long as your account is open. If you delete your account, we delete this data within 30 days.
  • Payment records: as long as the law requires (usually 6 years, for tax).

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • see the data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct it;
  • ask us to delete it;
  • ask us to send it to you or another service;
  • object to how we use it, or withdraw your consent.

To use any of these rights, email ngoclong003vn@gmail.com. We'll reply within one month. Parents can make requests on behalf of a child under 13.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. We'd appreciate the chance to sort it out first.

Keeping your data safe

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and passwords are never stored in plain text.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll tell you by email or with a notice on the site before they take effect.
