Inside the system

5 techniques backed by science

Not hacks. Not trends. Methods that neuroscience, psychology, and the world's best learners have used for decades.

01

Pomodoro+

25 minutes of focused work, 5 minute break. Scientifically proven to boost productivity without burnout. The "+" means we adapt it: 45-60 min blocks for deeper sessions.

Productivity + time management
02

Active Recall

Instead of re-reading, you test yourself. Doing questions from memory, writing down everything you know without notes. Statistically 300% more effective than passive study.

300% better retention rate
03

Spaced Repetition

Review material at increasing intervals — not all in one night. Works with the Forgetting Curve to keep information locked in long-term memory, not just for tomorrow's exam.

Long-term memory formation
04

3-Day Retention

Research shows memory retention drops sharply after 3 days. Stoday structures your review schedule around this window — so nothing falls into the "dead zone" and gets forgotten.

Prevents memory decay
05

Feynman Technique

Nobel physicist Richard Feynman: "If you can't explain it simply, you don't understand it well enough." Write what you know in plain words — it forces your brain to find the gaps.

Deep understanding, not surface recall
