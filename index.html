Why it works

Built on how memory actually works

No gimmicks. Stoday Plus is built around three of the best-researched study methods in learning science.

Spaced repetition

We forget much of what we learn within days unless we review it. Reviewing at growing gaps (day 1, 3, 7, 14, 28…) keeps it in long-term memory.

Active recall

Testing yourself beats re-reading notes. Pulling an answer out of memory is what makes it stick, which is why every tool asks you to produce answers.

Explaining to learn

Explaining a topic in simple words shows the gaps in your understanding fast. That's the idea behind the Feynman technique, and it's what Finley is for.
