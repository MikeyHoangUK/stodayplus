Technique 01

Pomodoro+

What it is — 25-minute focused blocks with a 5-minute break. Adapted to 45–60 min for deeper work sessions.

Why it works — Timed pressure activates focus. Planned breaks prevent the fatigue that quietly destroys retention.

25 min
Focus block — 25 min
Short break — 5 min
Deep session — 45–60 min
Outcome: sustained focus without burnout

Technique 02

Active Recall

What it is — Close your notes. Write or say everything you can remember. Then check and correct.

Why it works — Retrieval practice is up to 3× more effective than re-reading — because it makes your brain actually work.

Technique 03

Spaced Repetition

What it is — Review material at increasing intervals: Day 1 → Day 3 → Day 7 → Day 14.

Why it works — Each review resets the Forgetting Curve, moving information from short-term into long-term memory.

No review

1 review

Outcome: long-term retention

Technique 04

3-Day Retention

What it is — Review anything you've learned within 3 days — before it falls into the forgetting window.

Why it works — Memory drops sharply after 72 hours without review. One session inside that window resets the clock.

Technique 05

Feynman Technique

What it is — Explain what you've learned in plain language, as if teaching it to someone with no prior knowledge.

Why it works — Simplifying forces your brain to identify the parts you only think you understand — exposing real gaps.

1 — Learn

Study the topic normally

2 — Explain

Write it in your own words

3 — Find gaps

Where did you get stuck?

4 — Go back

Fill the gaps. Repeat.

Outcome: deep understanding, not surface recall
