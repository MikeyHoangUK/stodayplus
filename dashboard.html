01

Pomodoro+

25 min work + 5 min break. For deeper sessions: 45-60 min. Scientifically proven to sustain focus without mental fatigue.

Tip: Set a timer right now. The ritual starts the focus.
02

Active Recall

Close your notes. Write or say everything you can remember. Then check. This is 300% more effective than re-reading.

Use the Blurting Sheet to do this properly →
03

Spaced Repetition

Review material at increasing intervals. Day 1, Day 3, Day 7, Day 14. Fights the Forgetting Curve and locks info into long-term memory.

Schedule: revise every topic within 3 days of first learning it.
04

3-Day Retention

Memory plummets after 3 days if not reviewed. Structure your sessions so nothing falls into the dead window and gets erased.

Rule: if you learned it today, revisit it within 3 days.
05

Feynman Technique

If you can explain a concept in simple words to a 12-year-old, you understand it. If you can't, you don't — no matter how many times you've read it. Write your explanation. Find the gaps.

Exercise: Pick one topic. Explain it in 5 sentences without looking at your notes.

The Blurting Sheet

The simplest and most powerful active recall tool. Use it after every lecture, reading, or revision session.

How to use it

  1. 1Write the topic at the top. Close all notes.
  2. 2Blurt out everything you remember for 3–5 minutes. No peeking.
  3. 3Open your notes. Check and correct in a different colour.
  4. 4Note: what did you forget? What was wrong? That's your revision priority.

The Stoday Daily System

A repeatable daily structure. 30 minutes minimum. Follow this every day and you will outperform students doing 3-hour sessions.

1

Warm-up — 5 min

Blurt everything you remember from your last session. No notes. Just memory. This activates recall and shows you what's still in your head.

2

Deep Work — 20 min (Pomodoro+)

One topic only. New material or a deep review. Use the Feynman method: explain it back to yourself as if teaching someone else.

3

Review + Space — 5 min

Quick review of something you studied 3 days ago. This is your spaced repetition slot. Mark it in your notes. Schedule next review.

Total: 30 minutes. That's it.

Add more Pomodoro blocks if you have time. But 30 minutes done right is non-negotiable.
